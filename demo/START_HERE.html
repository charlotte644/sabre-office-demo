A good way to try it (5 minutes)

  1. Press Add sample orders in the bar along the bottom, so Steve's dashboard has something in it.
  2. Open the order page, add a few products, enter any name and any email address, and press Get my quote. Look at the inbox: the quote email is there.
  3. Press Accept on the quote, then pay (it is a demo payment). Watch the emails arrive: login and payment link, then the confirmation.
  4. Go back to the order page and tick I'd like Steve to visit and measure up first. Pick times. Then open Steve's dashboard, find it under Tasks, and approve a time. Only then does the client get their confirmation.
  5. In the dashboard, open Automations and press Send visit reminders now or Pretend 3 days have passed to see the automatic emails.

The bar along the bottom of every page switches between the client and Steve. Start again wipes the preview clean. Prices are the real sabreoffice.co.uk prices from the day this preview was made.
